Lab notes — Open WebUI for Mere Mortals
=======================================

Machine: 2 CPU, ~7.9 GB RAM, Ubuntu
24.04, Python 3.12.3. Everything ran
on loopback; nothing was exposed.

Versions: Open WebUI 0.12.3 (pip),
torch 2.14.1+cpu, Ollama 0.40.11
(official tarball), llama-cpp-python
0.3.36. Model: SmolLM2-135M-Instruct
GGUF, Q4_K_M, 105,454,432 bytes,
served two ways — by llama.cpp's
server (the OpenAI dialect) and by
Ollama (created from the same file).

What surprised the lab, in order:

1. The package index would not stay
   connected for large wheels (pip's
   downloads died, repeatably, around
   8 MB, and its resume returned zero
   bytes). curl moved the same files
   without trouble, so the lab
   resolved the tree once, fetched
   240+ wheels with curl, and
   installed offline. A sandbox
   property, not an Open WebUI one —
   but it is why t01 looks unusual.

2. The sandbox's own Ollama install
   was a shell: the CLI existed, its
   inference runner (lib/ollama) did
   not, and the sandbox later removed
   even that. The official tarball
   (1.5 GB through the same straw)
   supplied a working one.

3. The embedding model's first-start
   download failed on the sandbox's
   proxy variables ("Invalid port:
   ':1]'"). Files fetched with curl
   into the Hugging Face cache, plus
   a proxy-free start, loaded it.

4. Version 0.12.3 turns sign-ups OFF
   when the first (admin) account is
   created. Chapters 4 and 10 were
   corrected to the observed flow
   (t03, t05).

5. Sharing a preset is not enough:
   in 0.12.3 the person you share
   with also needs a grant on the
   base model, or their first chat
   with the preset answers "Model
   not found" (t05). The chapters
   say so now.

6. The 135M model, asked three times
   for exactly three words, produced
   "Hi!", "\"Hi, hello.\"", and
   "Hi! Hello! Hello!". Asked for a
   product it could have looked up
   in the attached tool, it invented
   two different wrong answers
   instead. Small models are honest
   about being small only in their
   scores, never in their confidence.

RAM notes: Open WebUI idles at a few
hundred MB with its embedding model
loaded; the whole lab (web UI, two
backends, tool server) coexisted in
under 2 GB. The pip install is the
heavy part — several GB of wheels.
